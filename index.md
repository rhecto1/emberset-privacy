# Emberset Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 17, 2026

Emberset is designed to keep your workout information private and under your control. This policy describes how the Emberset iOS and iPadOS app handles information.

## Information stored by Emberset

Information you enter into or import into Emberset is stored locally on your device. This may include:

- Workouts, completed sets, exercise history, and personal records
- Workout templates, training blocks, goals, progression settings, and notes
- Custom exercises, equipment and plate-calculator settings, and muscle-contribution profiles
- Bodyweight entries and other training measurements
- App settings and preferences
- Workout history you choose to import from Strong

Emberset does not require an account. Your workout information is processed on your device and is not automatically uploaded to the developer or an Emberset server. Emberset does not currently offer automatic cloud synchronization of workout history between devices.

## Data collection and tracking

Emberset does not include advertising, third-party analytics, or third-party tracking services. The developer does not sell your personal information or use your workout data for advertising.

The developer cannot access the workout or bodyweight information you store in Emberset unless you choose to send that information, for example in a support message or exported file.

The app includes bundled exercise illustrations and guidance; it does not need to send your workout information to the exercise-media provider to display them.

## App Store purchases and subscriptions

Optional Emberset Pro subscriptions are purchased and managed through Apple's App Store and StoreKit. Apple processes billing, subscription transactions, renewals, and refunds under Apple's own terms and privacy policies.

Emberset checks purchase information provided by Apple to determine whether Pro features are available. The developer may receive subscription and sales information made available by Apple through App Store Connect, but does not receive your payment-card details from Emberset.

Deleting your workout data or uninstalling Emberset does not automatically cancel an App Store subscription. You can manage subscriptions through your Apple Account settings.

## Rest notifications and Live Activities

Rest-timer notifications are scheduled on your device. If enabled, Emberset can show rest progress and relevant workout information in notifications, on the Lock Screen, and in the Dynamic Island through a Live Activity.

What other people can see depends on your device's notification, Lock Screen, and Live Activity settings. Emberset does not send workout information to the developer when displaying these features.

## Imports, backups, and exported files

You can import workout history from a Strong CSV file or restore an Emberset backup. These files are processed on your device.

You can export an Emberset backup containing workouts, templates, exercise settings, bodyweight entries, notes, and supported preferences. You choose where to save or share it. Once you export a file, the service you choose (such as Files, iCloud Drive, email, or another app) handles that copy under its own privacy practices.

Before replacing local data during a restore, Emberset may create an on-device recovery backup to help protect your existing records. Previously exported backups are not deleted when you delete information within Emberset.

Depending on your device and backup settings, Apple may include Emberset's locally stored information in an iPhone, iPad, or iCloud device backup.

## Support messages

If you email support, the developer receives the email address, message, and any files or screenshots you choose to include. This information is used to respond to your request. Please avoid attaching complete workout backups unless they are specifically needed for troubleshooting and you are comfortable sharing their contents.

## Data retention and deletion

Your workout information remains on your device until you change or delete it, choose **Delete All Data** in Emberset, or remove the app and its associated data.

**Delete All Data** removes the app's stored training records and related model-backed information. Some interface preferences may remain. It does not delete backup files you previously exported, change iOS permission settings, or cancel an App Store subscription.

## Security

Emberset relies on iOS and iPadOS data-protection and device-security features. Protect access to your device and any exported backup files, especially if they contain detailed personal training or bodyweight information.
